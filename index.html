Payroll cycle — 12-stage two-stream sequenceMonthly per entity
Stream 1 — HR Manager (Sujata)
1
Onboarding & CTC setup
New joiners active, CTC confirmed, Keka profiles live
2
Attendance management
Keka data synced, exceptions noted
3
Leave / WFH / OD
Approvals confirmed, LOP calculated
4
Attendance finalization
Final LOP locked — no further changes
5
Exit management — GATE
All exits must be excluded before this stage closes
Stream 2 — Finance Manager (Swati)
6
Compensation adjustments
Revisions, bonuses, arrears, holds — all FM-approved
7
Incentive validation
GMV vs AR reconciled · Joint FM + Sales Ops approval
8
Consultant payments
Contract-verified, approved, bank account designated
9
Final payroll review
FM reviews complete register before Keka submission
GATE
Keka submission gate
Stages 1–9 complete (or overridden) before Keka run enabled
10
Keka payroll run
Keka executes. Payslips generated.
11
Salary disbursement
Bank transfer executed. Confirmation received.
12
Cycle closure
Reconciliation complete. Actuals written to budget register.
